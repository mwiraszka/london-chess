The London Chess Club keeps only what it needs to run the club and this website. We never sell your information, and we will remove it if you ask.

Last updated October 30, 2026

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile photo, if you create an account.
  • Your member record: your name, ratings, city, Chess.com and Lichess usernames, FIDE and CFC IDs, email address, phone number, year of birth and the date you joined.
  • Club results: tournament results, standings and game scores from club events, under the players' names.
  • Tournament registrations: your name on the list of players registered for an upcoming tournament.
  • Photos: pictures taken at club events, with captions.

Who can see it

  • Anyone: member names, ratings, cities, Chess.com and Lichess usernames, FIDE and CFC IDs, member profiles, results, game scores, registrations, articles and photos.
  • The club's admins only: members' email addresses, phone numbers, years of birth and the dates they joined, which they use to run the club.

Emails we send

With an account, you get an email when your rating changes, and when an upcoming event is added, changed or removed. You can turn either off in your account's preferences.

Services that help run the site

  • Clerk handles accounts and logging in.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare stores the site's photos.
  • Sentry reports errors so we can fix them. A report includes your browser, your device and your IP address.
  • Plausible counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Google Maps shows where we meet. Loading the map lets Google know about your visit.

In your browser

The site remembers your settings, such as dark mode, in your browser. Logging in sets a cookie that keeps you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • To see, correct or remove your information, or to have a photo of you taken down, email us and we will take care of it.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your account page.

Contact us
