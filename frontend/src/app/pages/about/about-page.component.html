The London Chess Club is committed to providing a safe and welcoming environment for all members. We expect all members to treat each other with respect and kindness. Any form of harassment, discrimination, or bullying will not be tolerated and may result in expulsion from the club.

See the club's Code of Conduct and Bylaws documents for more information.

Chess tournaments are held throughout the year. These include casual blitz tournaments, rapid tournaments, and the London Chess Championship held in the fall.

Our blitz tournaments are unrated and free to play, with a time control of 10+0 (10 minutes for each player, with no increment after each move).

Our rapid tournaments are rated with a time control of either 25+0 or 40+0. For our rated tournaments there is a nominal entry fee (usually $10) that goes towards the prize pool and covers any expenses in organizing the event. See the schedule for a list of upcoming events.

Let the Tournament Director know when you arrive. They will help you get registered and answer any questions you may have about the event.

We use our own London & Region rating system, managed by . Current ratings are listed on the members page.

For tournaments we generally follow the policies and guidelines of the Canadian Chess Federation (CFC) and the laws of chess set by the International Chess Federation (FIDE). The notable difference is the touch-move rule: we enforce touch-move during rated tournaments but clock-move for our casual blitz tournaments.

Members are encouraged to try to resolve any disputes with their opponents using good sportsmanship before seeking arbitration. If you would like to speak to an arbiter, pause the clock and raise your hand to get their attention.

Every December we hold our Annual General Meeting (AGM), where we look back on the past year, review the club's finances, and elect a leadership team for the following year. All club members are welcome to attend the AGM. See the schedule for exact dates.

If you have any suggestions or would like to volunteer for a position, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at welcome@londonchess.ca or speak to or at the club.
